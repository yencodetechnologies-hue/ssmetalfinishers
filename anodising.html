About the Process

Built for Performance

Our aluminium anodising process transforms the component surface into a durable oxide layer engineered for demanding operating conditions.

Type III hard-coat anodising provides increased surface hardness, abrasion resistance and corrosion protection while retaining the lightweight properties of aluminium.

The process is suitable for precision components where coating consistency, dimensional control and repeatable finishing quality are critical.
