Aluminium Anodising

Specialized hard-coat aluminium anodising for aerospace-grade wear resistance and dielectric strength.

Overview

Precision Aluminium Anodising
Engineered to Spec

Type III hard-coat anodising converts the surface of aluminium components into a dense, wear-resistant oxide layer — dramatically improving hardness, dielectric strength, and abrasion resistance while keeping the part lightweight. Trusted by aerospace and precision optics manufacturers for demanding, weight-sensitive applications.
