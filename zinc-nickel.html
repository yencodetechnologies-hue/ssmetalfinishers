Overview

Precision Zinc Nickel Plating Engineered to Spec

Zinc-nickel alloy plating is the benchmark finish for automotive OEM fasteners and engine-bay components that must survive extreme temperature cycling and prolonged salt-spray exposure.

Our trivalent passivation process delivers over 1,000 hours of corrosion resistance while remaining fully RoHS and ELV compliant.
