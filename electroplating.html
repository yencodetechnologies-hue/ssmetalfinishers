01 / Overview

Precision plating. Engineered to spec.

Our electroplating process uses controlled electrolytic deposition to create a consistent metallic layer over your component surface.

The result is improved conductivity, corrosion resistance, surface durability and service life while maintaining the dimensional integrity of the underlying component.
